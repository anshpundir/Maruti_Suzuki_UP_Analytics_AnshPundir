# Maruti_Suzuki_UP_Analytics_AnshPundir
A data-driven business intelligence project analyzing Maruti Suzuki India Ltd.’s sales and customer performance across Uttar Pradesh (2024–2025) using MySQL. The project models complete dealership, sales, and customer datasets to generate insights on revenue growth, car model trends, payment behavior, and sentiment analysis.
This project is a comprehensive SQL-based business analytics study conducted on the Maruti Suzuki India Ltd. (MSIL) dataset focused on the Uttar Pradesh regional market. It demonstrates how structured data modeling, relational database design, and SQL queries can be used to derive actionable business intelligence insights from large-scale automobile sales and customer data.
The study covers data from 2024 and 2025, analyzing dealership performance, customer sentiment, and sales growth patterns across major cities including Lucknow, Kanpur, Noida, Agra, and Meerut. The relational schema includes multiple tables — Company, Region, Dealership, Car Model, Customer, Sales, and Review — representing real-world business relationships in Maruti Suzuki’s regional operations.
Through SQL-based queries, the project performs multi-dimensional analysis of sales, revenue, car model popularity, dealership efficiency, payment preferences, and customer satisfaction. Results are interpreted conceptually through a business intelligence dashboard framework, illustrating key metrics such as total sales, revenue by city, payment mode distribution, sentiment ratio, and model-level performance.
The insights reveal that Maruti Suzuki achieved approximately 20% year-over-year revenue growth, with SUVs like Brezza and Grand Vitara leading in both revenue and satisfaction ratings. Customer sentiment analysis showed 78% positive feedback and an average rating of 4.2/5, highlighting high brand trust and service reliability.
The dashboard interpretation integrates bar charts, pie charts, and KPI indicators to visualize monthly revenue trends, dealership rankings, and sentiment distributions. Findings were compiled into a detailed 20,000-word analytical report, providing managerial recommendations, strategic insights, and research interpretations on pricing, discounting, and dealership management.
Technologies used include MySQL for database design and SQL analytics, with visualization concepts inspired by Power BI and Tableau. This project demonstrates how data-driven strategies can optimize sales forecasting, improve customer engagement, and enhance profitability in the automotive industry.
